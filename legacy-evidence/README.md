# legacy-evidence — copies of files that lived only in `~/ico-collatz` (not a git repository)

**Why this exists.** The delivered FCVE ledgers (VCE-001, VCE-002 and their revisions) cite evidence files under `~/ico-collatz/...` (Phase 8 reproduction receipt, axiom audit, Phase 18 results, ...).
`~/ico-collatz` is **not under version control and is not backed up anywhere**; those events are legacy and carry no hashes, so a lost file could never be verified against a replacement.
On 2026-09-19 (before a planned wipe-and-reclone) the small files were **copied** here, unchanged. The originals were not moved or deleted.

**What is here.** `ico-collatz/<same relative path>` for: `evidence/`, `receipts/` (incl. the Phase 18 `correction-ledger.md`), `adversarial/`, `reports/`, `latex/`, `scripts/`, `logs/`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `README.md`, `research-question.md`, and the small files of
`experiments/independent-checker/` (results TSVs, stack samples that showed the real cause of the Gate 9 stall, monitor logs, checker configs and outputs). `MANIFEST.sha256` lists the sha256 of every file; `shasum -a 256 -c MANIFEST.sha256` verifies them.

**Path mapping.** A ledger event citing `~/ico-collatz/receipts/phase8-reproduction.md` is satisfied by `legacy-evidence/ico-collatz/receipts/phase8-reproduction.md`. `tests/test_bootstrap.py` asserts every externally cited file has its copy here.

**Deliberately NOT here** (regenerable or not ours): `*.export` files (~556 MB of lean4export output; the export for a theorem is regenerated by the audit), `targets/` (a clone of `tangentstorm/eliahou-collatz-bounds` plus tool builds; re-clone and `scripts/setup.sh`),
the `lean4lean/` and `nanoda_lib/` source trees under `experiments/`, the 2026-09-19 runs already kept in `../independent-checks/`, and any PDFs (third-party copyright).

**Caveat.** These are copies made after the fact. They are the best surviving record of the legacy evidence; they cannot prove the originals were unmodified before 2026-09-19.
